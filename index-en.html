Campaign KPI Analysis

I monitor and analyze key performance indicators to optimize budget, targeting and creatives. I work with data from Meta Ads, Google Ads and build dashboards in Excel / Power BI.

Cost per ClickCPC

How much you pay per click. I use it to compare ads, audiences and placements.

  • What I look for: differences across audiences, ads and placements.
  • Decision: reallocate spend when cost rises without improving traffic quality.
Cost per LeadCPL

How much each lead costs. Key for courses and services businesses.

  • What I look for: sustained increases relative to a lead's potential value.
  • Decision: review targeting, creative and landing-page conversion.
Click-through RateCTR

Clicks over impressions. Tells me if copy + design are working.

  • What I look for: declining relevance or creative fatigue.
  • Decision: test a new angle, message or format before increasing spend.
Return on Ad SpendROAS

Investment vs revenue ratio. Decides whether to scale or pause.

  • What I look for: relative profitability by campaign and funnel stage.
  • Decision: scale only where returns are sustainable.

Analytical capability

Business Performance & Decision Analysis

Beyond marketing, I review the economics behind every decision: how profitable an operation is, how customer acquisition cost compares to customer value, and where it makes sense to invest before executing any strategy.

Customer acquisition cost CAC

How much it costs to acquire a new customer, weighing total investment against customers gained.

Customer lifetime value LTV

How much a customer generates over the entire relationship, to decide how much is worth investing to acquire them.

Break-even point Break-even

From what sales volume an operation stops losing money and becomes profitable.

Profitability Margin

What percentage of each sale remains as profit after costs — key for pricing and discount decisions.

Pricing strategy Pricing

How to set prices based on perceived value, competition and demand elasticity.

Demand projection Forecast

Estimating future revenue and demand from trends, seasonality and historical data.
