Analytics

Campaign KPI Analysis

I monitor and analyze key performance indicators to optimize budget, targeting and creatives. I work with data from Meta Ads, Google Ads and build dashboards in Excel / Power BI.

Cost per ClickCPC

How much you pay per click. I use it to compare ads, audiences and placements.

  • Compare ad sets and placements.
  • Detect expensive creatives.
Cost per LeadCPL

How much each lead costs. Key for courses and services businesses.

  • Relate CPL to product price.
  • Adjust offer and landing.
Click-through RateCTR

Clicks over impressions. Tells me if copy + design are working.

  • Low CTR → refresh creatives.
  • Detects ad fatigue.
Return on Ad SpendROAS

Investment vs revenue ratio. Decides whether to scale or pause.

  • Track per campaign and ad.
  • Prioritize what's profitable.
